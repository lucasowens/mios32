Click = press/release. ⌥-click = latch (hold gestures); click again to release, or it lets go when the page changes (Menu + GP). Encoders: scroll or drag to turn, click to push. Keys: Space play/stop · Esc exit · ←→ < / > · [] datawheel · hold Shift = Matrix SHIFT. Paint (top bar): drag along the GP step keys / MatriX grid to tap each key once (start on an empty key = fill, on a lit one = erase). Windows: drag the title bar, resize from the corner, double-click a title to fit it to the screen width; Snap (top bar) lines windows up on a 20 px grid.
